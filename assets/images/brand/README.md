## 📐 The "LGS" Geometric Monogram Concept

The **LGS** monogram is a bespoke geometric mark constructed from pure architectural lines and balanced curves:
- **L**: The foundational vertical anchor stem and solid horizontal baseline.
- **G**: The upper precision circular arc wrapping into an inner horizontal datum.
- **S**: The dynamic fluid serpentine flow traversing through the mid-axis to connect the entire monogram into a single harmonious structure.

## 🎨 Color Specifications
- **Primary Mark**: Near-Black / Charcoal (`#18181B`)
- **Primary Background**: Warm Off-White (`#FAFAF8`)
- **Subtle Accent**: Editorial Lavender (`#7C6EE6` / `var(--color-accent-lavender)`)
- **Dark Background Mode**: Crisp Warm Light (`#FAFAF8` / `#FFFFFF`)
